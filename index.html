Why the Saudi market

The Kingdom's livestock sector brings local demand, government support and strategic location together in one place.

Food security

Producing meat, dairy and eggs is a national priority under Vision 2030, and herd health is the foundation of that production.

Constant demand for medicine and nutrition

Camels, sheep, cattle and poultry need treatment, prevention and nutrition programs all year round, not only in specific seasons.

Clear product regulation

The Saudi Food and Drug Authority registers veterinary products, and the Ministry of Environment, Water and Agriculture oversees the livestock sector.

A location that serves the region

A logistics gateway linking the Gulf, Africa and Asia, suited to manufacturers looking for a regional production and distribution base.
